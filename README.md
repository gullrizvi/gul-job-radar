# Gul Job Radar

An AI-powered personal job search platform.

## Goal

Find fresh remote and international job opportunities that match Gul's profile.

## Initial preferences

- Remote-first
- Global opportunities
- Pakistan-eligible remote roles
- USD, GBP or EUR compensation preferred
- Target income: $2,500–$3,000+ per month
- Seniority: Any level
- Full-time, part-time and contract opportunities
- Marketing
- Digital marketing
- Communications
- Content
- Growth
- Partnerships
- Fundraising
- Brand
- Campaigns
- Social media

## Preferred sectors

- NGOs
- INGOs
- International development
- Environment
- Conservation
- Healthcare
- Education
- Technology
- Fintech
- Foundations
- International organisations

## Important matching rule

Do not reject opportunities simply because the candidate is overqualified.

The user is intentionally open to junior and mid-level remote opportunities if the compensation, flexibility and work are suitable.
